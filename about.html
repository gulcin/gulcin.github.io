About

Hi, I'm Gülçin Yıldırım Jelínek.

Gülçin Yıldırım Jelínek · GUEL-chin YUHL-duh-rum YEH-lee-nek

I started working with Postgres at a startup in 2012 and was amazed by how powerful it is. I've stayed close to it ever since. Today I'm Product Lead on the PostgreSQL team at ClickHouse, which I joined in August 2026. Before that I worked at Xata and at EDB, and earlier at 2ndQuadrant, where I was Cloud Services Manager and led the cloud development work before EDB acquired the company in 2020.

The community is a big part of my Postgres life. I was elected to the PostgreSQL Europe Board in 2017, and I'm recognised as a PostgreSQL contributor. I'm a co-founder of Postgres Women, and since 2017 I've been the main organizer of the monthly Prague PostgreSQL Meetup. While I was at EDB, I hosted The Builders: A Postgres Podcast.

Outside Postgres, I co-founded Kadın Yazılımcı (Women Developers of Türkiye) in 2013 and still help lead it. In 2023 I started Diva: Dive into AI, an annual conference, and plans for 2027 are already set. I'm originally from Turkey and live in Prague.

Want me at your event or on your podcast? See how to invite me, or email gulcin.yildirim@clickhouse.com.
