Founder, organizer, committees

Community

The community is a big part of my Postgres life. I was elected to the PostgreSQL Europe Board in 2017, and I'm recognised as a PostgreSQL contributor. I advocate for diversity in the Postgres community and the wider tech world, and I was one of the early members of Postgres Women. Since 2017 I've been the main organizer of the monthly Prague PostgreSQL Meetup. While I was at EDB, I hosted The Builders: A Postgres Podcast.

Outside Postgres, I co-founded Kadın Yazılımcı (Women Developers of Türkiye) in 2013 and still help lead it. In 2023 I started Diva: Dive into AI, an annual conference, and plans for 2027 are already set.

Recognized weekly on postgres-contrib.org.
